Scope becomes an interaction

The reviewed ProjectEstimator component lets a visitor choose a project package, toggle add-ons, and select maintenance. It derives a one-time total, monthly amount, and first-year estimate from the current selections.

Separate the pricing decisions

Package, add-on, and maintenance data are separate collections. Totals are calculated from those records rather than manually repeated across the interface. This is a concrete example of reusable data driving a customer-facing interaction.

A complete presentation structure

The repository contains home, services, work, case studies, contact, and legal page implementations. This establishes page structure, not verified client engagements or the truth of every marketing claim contained in the source.

Why feature this project

The estimator is the strongest inspected exhibit: it makes scope tangible and explains a business decision through interaction. It complements Mow Magic’s operational depth and Everfold’s product architecture.
