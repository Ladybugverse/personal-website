One business. Several connected needs.

My responsibilities span website creation and management, brand identity and logo, content, social media, promotions, apparel, SOPs, custom applications, CRM, and business records. The work combines operational requirements with creative and technical delivery.

Public intake with a persistent record

The Next.js source implements service content, lead validation and persistence, campaign attribution, QR routes, and optional private photo intake. Notification handling preserves a saved lead when email delivery fails. That decision separates record integrity from notification availability.

An operational view of the customer

A separate team application contains Firestore-connected client, lead, property, calendar, estimate, invoice, expense, vendor, and equipment workflows. Structured property and service records represent information needed before and after a visit.

A lightweight estimate alternative

The branded microsite is a separate implementation. Its PHP endpoint validates requests, recalculates estimates on the server, and stores lead records. Public marketing and internal operations have different data and access needs; the repositories keep those concerns separate.

Brand and process belong in the system

My work also includes the visual identity, promotional materials, apparel, process documentation, and records. Shared styling and configurable service content support consistency across touchpoints.

What the evidence demonstrates

The deliverable is a body of implemented business software plus confirmed operational and creative responsibility. It demonstrates requirements translation, customer-record modeling, workflow design, and cross-functional ownership. Revenue gains, time savings, staff adoption, and end-to-end production performance have not been measured here.
